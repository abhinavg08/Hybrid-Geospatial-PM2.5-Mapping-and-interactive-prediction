1. METHODOLOGY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Architecture : Hybrid LUR (IDW-anchored, environmental predictors)
  Training data: IDW surface built from 14 CPCB station measurements
  Predictors   : Road network, land use, terrain, satellite (no coordinates)
  CV strategy  : Spatial block CV (4 geographic quadrants)
                 + Standard 5-fold KF CV for comparison
  Validation   : CPCB station comparison (naive)

  Scientific basis:
  The IDW surface encodes real PM2.5 spatial patterns measured by CPCB.
  The model learns which environmental features explain those patterns.
  Spatial block CV tests whether learned patterns generalize to
  unmeasured parts of the city (held-out geographic quadrants).
